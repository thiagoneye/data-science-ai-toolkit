# Scientific research standards

- Frame testable hypotheses with measurable objectives and limitations.
- Separate factual evidence, assumptions, exploratory observations and conclusions.
- Verify bibliographic metadata and never fabricate DOIs, papers or quotations.
- Compare against state-of-the-art fairly using matched datasets and budgets where possible.
- Report ablation, confidence intervals or per-case analyses when justified.
- Prioritize reproducible figures, tables, methods and documented computational costs.
